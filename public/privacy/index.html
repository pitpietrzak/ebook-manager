Privacy Policy

Biblioteczka Leniwców · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Biblioteczka Leniwców is a private, non-commercial e-book library used by a small group of family members and friends. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

Data we collect

  • Google account basics: your name, email address and profile picture, used to sign you in and show who added or reviewed a book.
  • Content you create in the app: book ratings, reviews, read status, folder and series names, and reports about book links.
  • Settings: your display name, Kindle email address and notification preference.
  • Device push token (mobile app only): used to send notifications about newly added books.

How we use Google data

The app requests access to the following Google services, and uses it only for these features:

  • Google Drive: to list, upload, download, move and organise e-book files in the shared library folder on Drive. The app does not read or modify other files in your Drive.
  • Gmail (send only): to send an e-book file to your own Kindle email address when you tap “Send to Kindle”. The app cannot read your email.

Google access tokens are used only to perform the actions you request. A Google refresh token is stored securely on the app's server (or on your device in the mobile app) so you don't have to grant access again each time. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Google: sign-in, Drive file storage and Gmail sending.
  • Netlify: hosting of the web app.
  • OpenAI: book metadata only (titles, authors, ISBNs) and search queries, to fill in book details, suggest folder icons and power smart search. No personal data is sent.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared with anyone outside the providers above. Other members of the library can see your display name together with the books you added and the ratings and reviews you wrote.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while you use the library. To have your account and data deleted, contact the app owner and it will be removed. Removing the app's access in your Google account stops all Drive and Gmail access immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app owner, Piotr Pietrzak.
